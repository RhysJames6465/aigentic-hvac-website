Practical questions

What HVAC owners usually want to know first.

What happens on the HVAC growth call?+

We review your current lead flow, missed opportunities, systems and capacity. You leave with a clearer view of the most commercially useful place to begin.

Do we need to replace our current CRM or field-service software?+

Usually not. We work around your existing setup wherever practical and confirm any integration requirements before implementation.

Which opportunity should we address first?+

We begin with the clearest measurable leak, such as missed calls, dormant enquiries, unapproved estimates, slow response or inconsistent follow-up.

How do you control what the AI says and when a person takes over?+

Messaging, qualification rules, exclusions, escalation points and human hand-offs are agreed with your team before anything goes live.

What information or system access will you need?+

That depends on the agreed starting point. We identify the minimum relevant data and access required before implementation, including any consent or compliance considerations.

What type of HVAC contractor is this best suited to?+

Established contractors with an existing flow of enquiries or customer records, capacity to accept more work and a willingness to track outcomes.

Are the calculator projections guaranteed?+

No. The calculator is an illustrative planning model. Actual results depend on lead quality, database condition, response capacity, offer strength and implementation.
